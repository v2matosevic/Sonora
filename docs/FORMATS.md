# Sonora formats and limits

Extensions, containers and codecs differ. This is representative coverage, not a promise for every file.

| Coverage | Formats | Conditions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sonora | MP3, WAV/PCM, FLAC, AAC/M4A, ALAC, Ogg Vorbis, Opus, AIFF | Rare layouts/profiles conditional; Windows shared-output processing; AAC/M4A padding not universally trimmed |

Windows shared audio does not promise bit-perfect output. AAC/M4A encoder padding can affect gapless playback. Physical output and perceptual quality depend on your device.
